In the middle of writing this, I suddenly felt that I actually no longer believed what I was writing. This will probably stay a draft.

I, like many other students, woke up today with a feeling of dread. I remembered that AP exam scores had come out (actually they came out yesterday, but I forgot about that).
This made me revisit a question that has long been debated among students: is standardized testing necessary?

I consider myself someone who is good at standardized tests, at least to some extent. I don't take too much stress over them and I am able to relatively easily parse questions. In the past, when my friends would complain about how hard the tests they were taking were, I would often quietly think to myself about how weird it was they felt this way. But, as time wore on, I too began feeling this way towards tests. I love the challenge they pose, but they do often feel quite arbitrary. I agree with my peers on that account, that the tests aren't a very good measure of understanding or skill at a certain subject, at least in the way they are commonly administered. However, I do think they have their place.

I feel that studying for and taking standardized tests can have a positive impact on students, and teach them some valuable skills, but I am still unsure if the skills that are taught are valuable enough to justify the drawbacks discussed earlier.
