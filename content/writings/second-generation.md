There are a million stories about immigrant families, their children, and how their children integrate or even assimilate into American society. I want a new kind of story. One where the child is able to embrace their culture authentically, and feel at home in their culture in American culture.

I didn't ever notice this as a child, but as I grew up I started to. My peers and I were almost all born in the United States, or at the very least raised there.
