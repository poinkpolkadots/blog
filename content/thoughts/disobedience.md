A couple of years ago I first watched the movie *Disobedience* (2017), directed by Sebastián Lello because I heard it was a sweet, heartbreaking story with a really good atmosphere. I really enjoyed watching it and I think it's one of the best movies I've watched recently (although I say that about a lot of things, so take that lightly).
This summer, I read the book *Disobedience* (2006) by Naomi Alderman because I had enjoyed the film so much and I wanted to see what the source material was like. I really liked the book, and in this post I want to outline the differences I see and what I think either the book or movie does better, because I think it's interesting.

First, I want to talk about what I think makes the film so great. When I first watched it, I was totally engrossed by the story; I found it fascinating. I loved how it gave a look into a community I had very little knowledge about without overexplaining or feeling inauthentic. They did a really good job at recreating the insights the author gave in the book about their feelings about the environment.
The acting in the movie is exceptional. They do a great job capturing a lot of the inner thoughts and monologue that are present in the book. I would also like to highlight the lead Rachels. They have amazing chemistry, and their acting is superb
