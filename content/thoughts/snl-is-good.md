I've been watching SNL since I was probably too young to be watching SNL. Everyone says their favorite cast is the one that they grew up with, I grew up watching Kate McKinnon, Beck Bennett, Kyle Mooney, Aidy Bryant, Vanessa Bayer, and Melissa Villaseñor, to name a few (I know, I'm young hehe). During this time, when I was watching SNL re-runs on YouTube I would see people say "SNL sucks" or "SNL hasn't been good in decades".
I never felt that way then, and I definitely don't feel that way now. I think SNL has only gotten better since I first started watching. The new style of comedy they have almost feels exclusively tailored to me, and I feel extremely blessed. Over time, I think SNL has leaned more into the absurd and irreverent styles of comedy. Of course, they've always done this to some extent, but I feel it even more now. The biggest example of this I can think of is the use of straight men to say silly things. No longer are straight men simply limited to pointing out the joke, now they can actually make jokes themselves!
Another thing that SNL is doing more commonly now is experiementing with the format of the show. Having more musical guests appear in sketches, having random cameos, making meta, 4th-wall-breaking references, and simply doing things that the audience doesn't expect. Taking advantage of the element of surprise has made SNL infinitely funnier, and will allow the show to contine for many more generations without becoming boring or tired.
I, personally, credit this shift mainly to Bowen Yang and Celeste Yim. I am forever grateful for their contributions to the SNL canon and hope their influence is felt for generations to come. I especially credit these two for introducing more modern, relevant, and funny pop culture to sketches, especially from the internet. In the past, when SNL tried to address modern pop culture/try to tap into what young people find funny, they would often come off as out of touch and "cringe". Now, it simply feels like they are adding to the conversation around these topics and extending the already existing jokes in a genious way.

In conclusion, I LOVE SNL. I love their style of humor, I love how commonly they subvert audience expectations, and I love the modern style of humor on the show.
